ABOUT ME

Designer. Former healthcare worker. Perpetual learner.

I’m Linda — a product designer based in the Boston area with 4+ years of experience building B2B SaaS and mobile products from the ground up.

Before design, I worked in healthcare as a physical therapy aide. That experience taught me to slow down, observe carefully, and actually listen before jumping to solutions. Turns out those habits translate really well to UX.

Most recently I was the founding designer at MykroGrid, a Nigerian clean-energy startup, where I owned design across four platforms — from a mission-critical operations dashboard to a warehouse management system — while building the company’s design system from scratch.

I care about work that’s thoughtful, well-crafted, and actually useful to the people using it. I’m drawn to complex problems, cross-functional teams, and products that make a real difference in how people work.

Off the clock I crochet, game, read, take long walks, and rotate between pickleball and basketball depending on who’s asking.

I’m currently open to full-time product design roles — remote or hybrid within the Boston area.
